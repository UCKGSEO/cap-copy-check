    How the score works

    This is not a correctness score. It shows how much of the checklist you have confirmed: 13 lines, 2 filled in by the scan and 11 for you. It moves in six bands on purpose, because a figure like 63% would suggest precision the checklist does not have.

    5%Nothing checked yet
    15%A couple of items confirmed
    25%Roughly a third done
    50%About half done
    75%Most of the way there
    95%Everything confirmed. The ceiling, on purpose

    The scale stops at 95% because sign-off should come from a person reading the copy, and from CAP Copy Advice where the risk is real.

    Written in the same structure as CAP Copy Advice replies so it is easy to compare. It is labelled as an internal automated note and must not be shared or described as advice from CAP or the ASA.

    Ask AI for a deeper read

    Choose an AI to copy a review prompt and open its website. The prompt includes your current draft, material type and CAP findings, and asks for risks and safer replacement wording. Paste it into the AI chat and submit it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically, and no API keys are needed.

    Before pasting, remove personal details and anything you do not have permission to share, especially health information. Once submitted, your draft is handled under that provider’s terms and privacy settings. An AI review is not CAP, ASA or legal approval.

    CAP Code rules and where they come from

    Rule numbers and wording were checked against the CAP Code and the ASA's religious and spiritual healing guidance in October 2026. The Code is updated from time to time, so confirm against the current text. The broadcast code (BCAP 15.12 and 15.13) says faith healing and miracle working must not be claimed to treat health problems; the ASA applies the same thinking to non-broadcast copy through rules 12.2 and 1.3.

    Sources: CAP Code (non-broadcast) · AdviceOnline: religious and spiritual healing · ASA ruling: Healing on the Streets, Bath

    This tool is an automated word and phrase scan. It cannot judge context, tone or images, and a clean result is not approval. CAP Copy Advice is free for UK advertisers and does not bind the ASA, but it is the right route for anything on the borderline.
